# Source notes — Knight Capital story

This narration is based primarily on the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's 2013 order against Knight Capital Americas LLC:

- SEC, *Knight Capital Americas LLC*, Release No. 70694 (Oct. 16, 2013): https://www.sec.gov/litigation/admin/2013/34-70694.pdf
- SEC summary of the enforcement action and findings: https://www.sec.gov/news/digest/2013/dig101613.htm

Key facts used in the narration: 212 incoming parent orders triggered the defective path; millions of child orders and more than four million executions across 154 stocks; over 397 million shares traded in about 45 minutes; roughly $460 million in losses; 97 automated emails mentioning the router issue before the market opened; and a $12 million SEC settlement in 2013.

Wording note: the SEC describes millions of child orders and more than four million executions. The narration avoids claiming that the system sent exactly four million distinct incoming customer orders.
